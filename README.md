## What FallVet Does

FallVet provides veterinary teams with a compact workflow workspace that does not depend on a hosted service. The application is distributed as a standalone HTML file, allowing it to be launched in a browser from local storage and used without an active connection.

Its workflow covers common practice activities, including animal records, appointment coordination, prescribing, and compliance-focused documentation. Since the application follows a local-first model, information remains on the device and deployment can stay simple and browser-based.

## Working with FallVet

After launching the file, FallVet serves as a local browser workspace for veterinary practice operations.

A typical sequence may include:

1. Create new animal records or inspect existing ones.
2. Organize upcoming appointments and maintain the schedule.
3. Add or revise prescribing information.
4. Enter controlled substances activity as applicable.
5. Complete the relevant compliance fields and logs.
6. Use the machine-callable integration to connect external tools when needed.

All changes are managed on the device used to open the application, consistent with its local-first design.
